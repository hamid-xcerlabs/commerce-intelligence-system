# Business Problem

## Overview

Retail brands, especially boutique and direct-to-consumer businesses, rely heavily on conversational channels (e.g. Instagram DMs) to engage with customers.

However, these interactions are often unmanaged and unstructured.

---

## Core Issues

### 1. Delayed Responses

- Manual handling leads to slow reply times
- Customers lose interest or move to competitors

---

### 2. Missed Opportunities

- High-intent messages are overlooked
- No prioritization of valuable leads

---

### 3. Lack of Personalization

- Generic replies fail to match customer expectations
- No understanding of preferences (size, style, intent)

---

### 4. No Follow-Up System

- Conversations end after initial response
- No mechanism to re-engage interested users

---

### 5. Fragmented Tools

- Messaging, product data, and operations are disconnected
- No unified intelligence layer

---

## Business Impact

These problems result in:

- Lower conversion rates
- Poor customer experience
- Revenue leakage from warm leads
- Increased operational inefficiency

---

## Market Reality

Modern customers expect:
- Fast responses
- Personalized recommendations
- Seamless interactions

Most small-to-mid retail brands fail to meet these expectations due to lack of systems.

---

## Opportunity

There is a clear gap for systems that can:
- Structure conversations
- Automate decision-making
- Connect intent to action

---

## Summary

The absence of an intelligent system to manage customer conversations leads to lost revenue and poor customer engagement.
