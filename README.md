# Commerce Intelligence System

AI-powered system designed to convert customer conversations into real-time product recommendations and revenue opportunities for modern retail brands.

---

## Problem

Retail brands lose revenue due to:
- Delayed or missed customer responses
- Lack of personalized product recommendations
- No structured follow-up after customer intent

---

## Solution

This system transforms inbound customer messages into:
- Context-aware responses
- Real-time product matching
- Automated follow-ups based on purchase behavior
